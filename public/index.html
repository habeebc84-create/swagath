Help

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open daily and close at 10:30 pm. Call 083400 03444 to confirm festival or special timings.

Do you have pure vegetarian options?

Yes — a full veg menu including soups, starters, rice, and meals. We also serve non-veg (chicken, mutton, seafood). Tell staff if you need pure-veg handling preferences.

Can I book a table online?

Yes — use the Book Table form on this site or WhatsApp / call us. We will confirm by phone when needed.

Do you do catering and bulk orders?

Yes — family packs, office parties, and events. Minimum plates and 24–48 hrs notice usually apply. See the Catering section or WhatsApp us.

Is the restaurant air-conditioned?

Yes — Swagath Multicuisine is an A/C restaurant with family-friendly seating.

How do I order on WhatsApp?

Tap the green WhatsApp button, send your items and quantity, and our staff will confirm. No online payment on the website — pay at the restaurant or as agreed for bulk orders.

Where are you located?

Kalikiri Cross Road, MPL, Kalikiri, Andhra Pradesh 517234 (Plus Code MR32+5R). Open the map in the Contact section for directions.
