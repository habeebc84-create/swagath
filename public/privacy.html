Privacy Policy

Swagath Multicuisine Restaurant A/C · Kalikiri · Updated 4 October 2026

This policy explains how we collect and use information when you visit our website, book a table, or request catering.

1. Information we collect

  • Name, phone number, and optional email when you submit a reservation or catering form
  • Party size, date, time, event details, and messages you write
  • Technical data such as browser type (standard server logs), if applicable

2. How we use it

  • To confirm bookings, catering, and orders
  • To call or WhatsApp you about your request
  • To improve service and respond to complaints

We do not sell your personal data. We do not run online payments on this website.

3. Sharing

Data is accessible only to authorised restaurant staff. We may share information if required by law or food-safety / consumer authorities.

4. Retention

Booking and catering records are kept only as long as needed for operations and legal compliance, then deleted or anonymised.

5. Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of your booking data by calling 083400 03444. We follow principles consistent with India’s digital personal data protection framework for reasonable care of guest information.
