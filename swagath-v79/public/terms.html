Indian allergen rules recognise major allergens (including cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk, tree nuts, sesame, mustard, and others notified by FSSAI). We take reasonable care but cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free kitchen environment where cross-contact may occur.

  • If you believe food is unsafe or unfit, stop consumption and inform the manager immediately so corrective action can be taken.
